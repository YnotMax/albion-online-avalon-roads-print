Whitewall Pass

T8Black ZoneMountainMorgana

Whitewall Pass is a Tier 8 black zone in Albion Online. It is located in the Mountain biome, Outlands region. Full-loot PvP is always active with no flagging required. Gatherable resources include Ore, Stone, and Fiber.

PvP Rules

Full-loot PvP at all times — no flagging needed. Any player can be attacked on sight. Highest risk, highest reward.

Resources

Resource tiers T2–T8 spawn in this zone.

OrePrimaryStoneSecondaryFiberTertiary

Faction

This zone is populated by Morgana mobs. Open-world mobs and dungeons in this area feature Morgana enemies.
